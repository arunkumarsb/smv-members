About Sir M Visvesvaraya

This layout is named in honour of Sir Mokshagundam Visvesvaraya (1861–1962), the celebrated engineer and statesman who served as Diwan of Mysore and designed the Krishna Raja Sagara Dam. Awarded the Bharat Ratna in 1955, his birthday, September 15, is observed across India as Engineer's Day.
